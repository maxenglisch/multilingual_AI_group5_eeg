Note on methodology. Band limits and assignments are typical literature values and differ slightly between sources. Gamma (>30 Hz) is strongly EMG-prone with this setup; frontopolar channels are rich in artefacts. Mu and SMR have no band of their own in EmotivPRO and are mapped to alpha and low beta respectively; delta is not exported at all.
Extending: States live in states.json – the Python side uses the same file. The STATES block at the bottom of the <script> must be kept identical to it.
Recording mode: Scores come from flex2_export_json.py. Segments that do not pass the quality gate (CQ.Overall < 60 or EQ.SampleRateQuality = −1) are deliberately not classified.
